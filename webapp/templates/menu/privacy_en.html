About this policy

Sievers Study Hall is an online application for practising German grammar. This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the application.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact sieversstudyhall@gmail.com.

Information we process

When you create an account or use the application, we may process:

  • Account information needed to register and sign in, such as your email address and account identifier.
  • Learning information, such as completed exercises, submitted answers, mistakes, bookmarks and progress.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the website, potentially including IP addresses, browser information and server logs.

Why we process information

We use information to provide accounts, remember learning progress and preferences, evaluate answers, maintain the application, troubleshoot problems and protect the service against misuse.

AI-assisted exercise feedback

Some exercises use an external AI service to evaluate submitted answers and generate feedback. The text you submit, together with relevant exercise instructions, may be sent to the service provider for processing.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in exercise answers.

Hosting and service providers

Sievers Study Hall is hosted by PythonAnywhere, a hosting provider operated by PythonAnywhere LLP, based in the United Kingdom.

The application is hosted on servers located in the United States. PythonAnywhere may process personal data, including IP addresses, server logs, account information, and saved exercise progress, as necessary to provide its hosting services.

For more information, please consult PythonAnywhere's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and local storage

The application may use cookies or similar browser storage to maintain sign-in sessions and remember settings. You can manage cookies through your browser, although blocking essential cookies may prevent some features from working.

How long information is kept

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Where applicable, you may also request data portability or complain to a competent data-protection authority.

To make a request, email sieversstudyhall@gmail.com.

External websites

Links to external websites, including Substack, are governed by those websites' own privacy policies. This policy does not cover their independent processing of your data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data-processing practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
